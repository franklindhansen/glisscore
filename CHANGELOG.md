# Changelog

## 1.2.27

Turning off Shadows now leaves the turning page unshaded. Page transition's length slider is just called Length, and groups with nothing to act on dim as one. The zoom buttons look active when usable.

## 1.2.26

Even file tiles, shared Bold and Italic and zoom groups, and a custom color field. Closed settings groups are better balanced, and the Shadows switch stays at the right.

## 1.2.25

The video editor's settings now sit in tiles, with Shadows laid out in one-line rows and a Reset beside its switch. Background and Ink color are one field with a color picker, and the PDF Editor's Clean, Crop, and Arrange use cards. Glass bars share one look.

## 1.2.24

Buttons, fields, menus, tips, and windows now share one set of spacing and sizes, with small shifts of a pixel or two. In the video editor, settings that belong to another sit plainly under it, and the back-to-start button matches Play.

## 1.2.23

The video editor's panel, timeline, and full-screen controls now use the same spacing and sizes as the other editors. The Fades curve choice sits under its picture, and the timeline bar fits in two rows on small screens.

## 1.2.22

The PDF Editor's small layout now matches the Audio Editor: tools open from one attached bar with Apply beside the name. Clean's Specks is now Strength, and the page position reads 1 / 3.

## 1.2.21

The PDF Editor fits cleanly at every window width, and its status lines are shorter.

## 1.2.20

On small screens the PDF Editor's tools open from a drop-down, and the page size slider floats over the pages and returns to the middle on a double-click. Select all and Deselect all sit in one pill, and the tool descriptions are shorter.

## 1.2.19

Re-size shows its page sizes and Page shape in two cards, and the Custom blanks now fit what you type. The PDF Editor's settings have even space above and below.

## 1.2.18

Watermark is laid out like the Audio Editor, and Custom direction and Custom size now turn their word into a blank you type in.

## 1.2.17

The Audio Editor looks the same from tool to tool. The Level warning note now reads better in dark mode, and the Imager's Both, Middle, and Sides choice is no longer cut off in narrow windows.

## 1.2.16

The Audio Editor's spacing is more even from tool to tool. The EQ band also switches layouts sooner, so its Gain and Width sliders stay wide enough on screens around 1000px.

## 1.2.15

The Emulator's graph now fits the music instead of running off the edge, and its summary and buttons are simpler and line up. EQ Match has shorter button names, and the Audio Editor's text sizes and buttons are more consistent.

## 1.2.14

EQ Match, the Emulator, and the EQ's Dynamic Auto now show their progress as a Processing note over the graph, and their buttons keep a steady size. The Dynamic row lines up, and the start cards no longer jump back after a hover.

## 1.2.13

The Audio Editor's buttons now match the video editor's, including a Hide tools button in full screen. Tools that need more room scroll instead of being cut off, and Spectra, Fades, and Level fill their window at every size.

## 1.2.12

Ogg and M4A files now come in and go out at their exact length, and M4A exports fall back to a lower bitrate with a notice when the browser can't do the one chosen. The spectrogram shows high tones at their true brightness.

## 1.2.11

Level is simpler, with Gain shown once and clearer notes. Number boxes and menus now share one look, and Spectra and Trim silence show an icon or a word, never both.

## 1.2.10

Noise reduction now picks its method with an Auto or Manual choice, with Learn doing the work for either. Remove hum and Output noise are simple switches, and Sensitivity and Smoothing are tidier.

## 1.2.9

In now sits above Noise reduction, so the whole chain reads from In to Out, and the cable plugs into Noise reduction and Spectra like every other tool. The idle message now reads "No processing."

## 1.2.8

The Audio Editor moves more consistently: fades, slides, and the Imager's Overall and Frequency switch now share the same timing and feel.

## 1.2.7

MP3 files now start cleanly in Safari instead of losing their first moment.

## 1.2.6

The Audio Editor has a fresh layout in every tool: a clearer tool list, Noise reduction in one row of steps, and Fades with two curves, Gradual and Straight (your old curves play as the nearest one). Sliders hold still as their numbers change, and the editor opens without stray motion.

## 1.2.5

The Audio Editor's tool windows are tidier, with even space around cards, headings, and sliders, and the Imager and EQ grids now show in dark mode. Bypass is saved with your settings and applies to exported audio.

## 1.2.4

The playhead is easy to grab on the timeline without moving page turns, and it lights up softly when you hover it. The page or system under it is shaded as you drag.

## 1.2.3

The System page selector now changes with the page turn, and System finds every staff on dense part pages. System also keeps the Play button in view in short windows.

## 1.2.2

System keeps your turns, systems, and preview steady as you edit, and gains a Letterbox switch. Loading pages show one clear pill in every layout.

## 1.2.1

The "How it works" dropdowns open faster with a soft settle, and their arrow no longer jumps when you hover over it.

## 1.2.0

System now finds your pages once, shows a quieter timeline, and lets you set any page to Full page.

## 1.1.22

The System timeline now shows one block per page, with a skinnier handle for each system turn inside it, and clears the thumbnails with one smooth wipe while pages load. Changing the margins no longer makes the preview flash.

## 1.1.21

General improvements and polish.

## 1.1.20

A page that is still being made now shows a softened preview with a sweep and a label, instead of a blank white sheet.

## 1.1.19

The selector now shows the music bright with the padding as frosted glass, marking is always on, and the preview frame stays fixed.

## 1.1.18

Marked systems now stay with their page: an edited page is found again and says so, and marks are saved with your settings.

## 1.1.17

Finding systems now shows its progress, can be stopped, and warns about tilted pages and two-page scans.

## 1.1.16

The System layout now lets you mark systems by hand, resize, move, and delete them, adjust the padding around each one, and detect a page again.

## 1.1.15

Every scrolling area, including How it works, the history lists, and the font lists, now has the same thin scrollbar.

## 1.1.14

The loading bars for fonts, files, and noise reduction now share one smooth sweep that starts at the left edge and finishes its pass. The README now names the fonts the site uses today.

## 1.1.13

Looking up a credits font now shows a thin loading bar instead of a line of text that nudged the panel. Hovering Save current frame previews the frame it will save, and the preset font list is tidier.

## 1.1.12

Dropping a page after reordering now glides into place smoothly. Your browser is also now set to only let this page talk to its own server.

## 1.1.11

The About and How it works windows are roomier and more consistent with the others, with small touches as they open.

## 1.1.10

The PDF Editor and Audio Editor now share one layout, and Noise reduction and Imager fit on a laptop screen.

## 1.1.9

Buttons and choices across the app now share consistent sizes, and the How it works headings are simpler.

## 1.1.8

The video settings are easier to scan: labels sit closer to their controls, Page colors has its own group, and First page alone sits under Spread. The folded Credits line shows what you typed, and each line's font list opens in full.

## 1.1.7

The app has a new font, Hanken Grotesk. The Credits panel is easier to use, with an Aa button on each line for its own font, bold, and italic, and the glisscore wordmark now asks before it clears your work.

## 1.1.6

Warning and reason text is easier to read. The title box now fits its text instead of jumping wider when you click it, and the (i) buttons are easier to hit.

## 1.1.5

The How it works text is shorter and clearer, and the button names in it now look like the real buttons.

## 1.1.4

Leaving full screen is smoother in the Audio Editor and the video editor.

## 1.1.3

Manual crop now works one page at a time, with Apply to selected to copy a frame to other pages. Buttons that are greyed out explain themselves more consistently.

## 1.1.2

Buttons press in when you click them, and the drop screen fades away smoothly. The full screen button and the intro button width are fixed.

## 1.1.1

Letting go of a start or end bracket no longer jumps the timeline when you are zoomed out. Full screen shows the zoom controls above the play bar, and Dim's wording is clearer.

## 1.1.0

A new Dim tool in the PDF Editor visually softens music that repeats. The video stage gets zoom controls, the start and end brackets glide smoothly, and Support now lives in About.

## 1.0.2

Save thumbnail never saves a file over YouTube's 2 MB limit. Site copy gets Oxford commas.

## 1.0.1

Site pages (/about, /how-it-works, /support) open correctly. How it works has clearer wording, and its dropdowns no longer shift the window.

## 1.0.0

The first full release: the PDF Editor, the Audio Editor and the Video Editor, with the Support dialog, the About dialog and the site pages.

## 0.6.3

The numbers of a big move start within 600ms, however many change, and only the numbers that really change take a turn.

## 0.6.2

A dropped page gives a small press, and the pages it passed slide into place with it. A big move starts its numbers sooner, and the numbers follow their cards up and down a row as well as sideways.

## 0.6.1

The number under a card now slides the way its card moves, sideways or up and down a row.

## 0.6.0

Motion is quicker and more consistent. The video player's full screen now glides in and out instead of jumping, and the Audio Editor's full screen is tighter, with no empty moment in the middle. Switching tools in the Audio Editor and PDF Editor is a quick fade with a small lift instead of a window-high slide, and a switch made mid-motion carries on from where it is. Buttons answer a press right away, hovers swell less, the toast, menu, and pop-ups are shorter, and hover effects no longer stick on touch screens.

## 0.5.1

Long scanned PDFs use far less memory, so a 220-page scan no longer pushes the browser to reload the tab as easily. Page cards far from the view free their pictures and draw again as you scroll, the browser keeps fewer remembered page pictures, and the file is held once. The page-turn button now reads Add page turn, as in the shortcuts, and a greyed Revert no longer puts "Nothing to revert." on the tool's status line; it is a tooltip.

## 0.5.0

Wrap is gone, and with it the Turn style choice. A Single page turn is always Peel now: the page lifts and curls from its free edge and is carried off to the left. Videos and settings files saved with a turn style load without it.

## 0.4.0

Wrap now turns the page once around its left edge, like a notebook folded in half: it swings out, lingers fully open, then folds back behind the next page. A faint fold line keeps it attached, and its shadows follow what is happening.

## 0.3.1

The PDF Editor's loading cards now share one sweep across the grid, and the large view's page box and the crop frame glide more smoothly.

## 0.3.0

Wrap is a new turn style for Single page: the page folds over its left edge and lands behind the next one. It replaces Fade; videos saved with Fade load as Peel.

## 0.2.0

History in the video editor now records every output setting as a named step, including Restore defaults and importing settings.

## 0.1.0

First numbered release: the Audio Editor, PDF Editor, and video editor, with a Single page layout and Turn, Slide, and Peel page turns.
