# Changelog

## 0.3.0

Wrap is a new turn style for Single page: the page folds over its left edge and lands behind the next one. It replaces Fade; videos saved with Fade load as Peel.

## 0.2.0

History in the video editor now records every output setting as a named step, including Restore defaults and importing settings.

## 0.1.0

First numbered release: the Audio Editor, PDF Editor and video editor, with a Single page layout and Turn, Slide and Peel page turns.
