# Changelog

## 1.1.2

Buttons press in when you click them, and the drop screen fades away smoothly. The full screen button and the intro button width are fixed.

## 1.1.1

Letting go of a start or end bracket no longer jumps the timeline when you are zoomed out. Full screen shows the zoom controls above the play bar, and Dim's wording is clearer.

## 1.1.0

A new Dim tool in the PDF Editor visually softens music that repeats. The video stage gets zoom controls, the start and end brackets glide smoothly, and Support now lives in About.

## 1.0.2

Save thumbnail never saves a file over YouTube's 2 MB limit. Site copy gets Oxford commas.

## 1.0.1

Site pages (/about, /how-it-works, /support) open correctly. How it works has clearer wording, and its dropdowns no longer shift the window.

## 1.0.0

The first full release: the PDF Editor, the Audio Editor and the Video Editor, with the Support dialog, the About dialog and the site pages.

## 0.6.3

The numbers of a big move start within 600ms, however many change, and only the numbers that really change take a turn.

## 0.6.2

A dropped page gives a small press, and the pages it passed slide into place with it. A big move starts its numbers sooner, and the numbers follow their cards up and down a row as well as sideways.

## 0.6.1

The number under a card now slides the way its card moves, sideways or up and down a row.

## 0.6.0

Motion is quicker and more consistent. The video player's full screen now glides in and out instead of jumping, and the Audio Editor's full screen is tighter, with no empty moment in the middle. Switching tools in the Audio Editor and PDF Editor is a quick fade with a small lift instead of a window-high slide, and a switch made mid-motion carries on from where it is. Buttons answer a press right away, hovers swell less, the toast, menu, and pop-ups are shorter, and hover effects no longer stick on touch screens.

## 0.5.1

Long scanned PDFs use far less memory, so a 220-page scan no longer pushes the browser to reload the tab as easily. Page cards far from the view free their pictures and draw again as you scroll, the browser keeps fewer remembered page pictures, and the file is held once. The page-turn button now reads Add page turn, as in the shortcuts, and a greyed Revert no longer puts "Nothing to revert." on the tool's status line; it is a tooltip.

## 0.5.0

Wrap is gone, and with it the Turn style choice. A Single page turn is always Peel now: the page lifts and curls from its free edge and is carried off to the left. Videos and settings files saved with a turn style load without it.

## 0.4.0

Wrap now turns the page once around its left edge, like a notebook folded in half: it swings out, lingers fully open, then folds back behind the next page. A faint fold line keeps it attached, and its shadows follow what is happening.

## 0.3.1

The PDF Editor's loading cards now share one sweep across the grid, and the large view's page box and the crop frame glide more smoothly.

## 0.3.0

Wrap is a new turn style for Single page: the page folds over its left edge and lands behind the next one. It replaces Fade; videos saved with Fade load as Peel.

## 0.2.0

History in the video editor now records every output setting as a named step, including Restore defaults and importing settings.

## 0.1.0

First numbered release: the Audio Editor, PDF Editor, and video editor, with a Single page layout and Turn, Slide, and Peel page turns.
