    How it works

    Everything happens inside your browser, on your own device. Your score and audio are never uploaded.

    Your files stay with you

    Your files are read into this tab’s memory. Nothing goes to a server, and closing the tab clears them. The only thing fetched from outside the site is a Google Font, and only when you pick one for the credits.

    Your score

    PDF.js, from Mozilla, draws each page. Your changes are a list of steps on top of the original PDF, which is never touched. Export PDF builds a new file from that list.

    How the PDF Editor works

    Click the file name under the title to open it. Tick the pages you want, then press a tool’s button. Each tool only works on the ticked pages, except Dim.

    Arrange

    Drag pages to reorder them. Click a page to leave it out.

    Clean

    Whitens the paper and removes specks. Revert puts the pages back.

    Re-kilter

    Straightens crooked scans.

    Crop

    Finds the music and draws a frame around it. Auto sets the frames. Manual lets you drag them yourself.

    Dim

    Visually softens parts of a page to draw less attention. Draw a box over sections to visually soften them, such as the extra music around a repeat on a duplicated page. Drag a box’s edges to adjust it, and press its X to remove it. Revert removes every box. Dim needs no ticks: draw on any page.

    Split spreads

    Cuts a two-page scan into two pages.

    Re-size

    Fits every page to a paper size.

    Watermark

    Prints your text across the ticked pages.

    Your audio

    Your browser reads the audio, and the waveform is measured on your device. What you hear while editing is what you export. Long recordings are kept in your browser’s private file storage, so they don’t have to fit in memory.

    How the Audio Editor works

    Click the audio file’s name under the title to open it. Its tools are listed on the left.

    Pipeline

    Sound runs through the modules from top to bottom. A lit module is active. Bypass takes out only the open module.

    Noise reduction

    Learn noise from a stretch with only hiss in it. Auto finds that stretch for you. Manual lets you pick it.

    Spectra

    Press Draw, then mark a Box, a Band or a Moment on the spectrogram. Heal fills it in from the sound around it. Reduce turns it down.

    EQ

    Bands can be Bell, Low shelf, High shelf, High-pass, Low-pass, or Notch. EQ Match fits bands to the difference between your recording and a reference.

    Imager

    Width makes the stereo image wider or narrower.

    Level

    Gain changes the volume. Normalize sets a target loudness. The Limiter keeps peaks under the ceiling, so nothing clips.

    Fades

    Fade the start in and the end out.

    Emulator (beta)

    Emulate measures another recording’s tone and loudness, then moves your EQ, width, and level toward it.

    History

    Each editor keeps its own undo steps, and you can go back to any one.

    Page turns

    Each turn is drawn in 3D by your graphics card. Drag a turn into the next one and that one gets pushed along. Hold Shift and click to select several at once. Turns to end packs the turns after the playhead against the end of the video. Add page turn places them in order. In Single page layout, each page gets its own turn, centered in the frame. Style chooses between Turn, Slide, and Cut.

    Your video

    Your browser renders and encodes every frame. Unless you pick Real-time recording, the sound is stored losslessly (24-bit) and the video goes into a MOV file. Real-time recording compresses the sound and saves an MP4 or WebM file. On Automatic, if fast export isn’t available, the video is recorded in real time instead.

    Saved settings

    Settings stay in this browser. Import / Export saves them to a small text file you keep.
