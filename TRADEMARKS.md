# *glisscore™* names, logos and the official project

The code of *glisscore* is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE), and you are free to use, study, change and share it under that license. The license does not give you the right to use the project's name or look as your own. As that license permits (section 7), I keep those rights.

This page says what that means in practice. It is a policy, not a registration: *glisscore™* is an unregistered mark that I use and enforce.

## What is reserved

- The name *glisscore*, in any spelling, case or close lookalike
- The logo, wordmark, favicons and icons in `brand/` and in the app
- The domain glisscore.com
- The words "official" and "official release" next to the name

## The official project

These, and only these, are the official *glisscore*:

- Site: [glisscore.com](https://glisscore.com)
- Source: [github.com/franklindhansen/glisscore](https://github.com/franklindhansen/glisscore)
- Support: [glisscore.com/support](https://glisscore.com/support)

Anything else, including a copy that looks the same, is not the official project.

## What you may always do

You do not need permission to describe the project truthfully. For example:

- Link to it, review it, write about it, criticize it, teach with it, make tutorials.
- Say that something works with *glisscore* or opens its files.
- Credit it: "Based on *glisscore*", "A fork of the *glisscore* project", "Powered by *glisscore*".
- Keep every copyright, license and attribution notice the AGPL requires.

## Forks and modified versions

If you publish a modified version, or run a modified version as your own service, then:

1. Give it a clearly different primary name, and use your own logo and visual identity.
2. Remove "official" statements, badges and the *glisscore* logo from it.
3. Say plainly that it is modified, and link to the original.
4. Do not suggest that I sponsor, approve or endorse it.
5. Keep the AGPL, its source offer, and the copyright and attribution notices.

A name like "AriaScore, based on *glisscore*" is fine. A name like "Glisscore Pro", "Glisscore Cloud" or "glisscore 2" is not, because it reads as the official project.

## What needs my permission

Using the name or logo as the main brand of anything other than the official project, for example a product, a hosted service, an app-store listing, a domain or a social account. Ask through [franklindhansen.com/contact](https://franklindhansen.com/contact). If I agree, I'll say so in writing.
